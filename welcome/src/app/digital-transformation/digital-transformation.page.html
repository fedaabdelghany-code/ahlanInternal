Digital Twin Model Builder

The Digital Twin is the visual nerve center for Sokhna plant operations. It combines a 3D model with live data so teams can understand the plant, make faster decisions, and reduce operational friction.

  • Viewer: Interactive 3D plant map built on Google Maps and in-house 3D models
  • Layered Data: Production KPIs, cameras, GPS, high-risk jobs, and safety heat maps with near-real-time BigQuery data
  • Multi-Tenant Admin: Manage tenants, users, roles, rules, projects, twins, and data sources
  • Builder: Create 3D site replicas, plan facility layouts, and maintain equipment inventory
  • Safety Intelligence: AI highlights PPE non-compliance and zone breaches from camera streams

Security Violations Detection
